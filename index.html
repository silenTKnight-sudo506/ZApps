Development Philosophy
01 / PRINCIPLE

Zero Bloat

Every line of code serves a purpose. No unnecessary background tasks, no telemetry, and absolute respect for device battery life.

02 / PRINCIPLE

Card-Driven UI

Moving away from text-heavy spreadsheets. Interfaces should be visual, intuitive, and built for modern tactile navigation.

03 / PRINCIPLE

Local First

Your data belongs on your device. Z Apps are designed to work flawlessly offline, prioritizing local storage indexing over cloud dependency.
