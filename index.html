Development Philosophy
01 / PRINCIPLE

Zero Bloat

Every line of code should have a reason to exist. No unnecessary background tasks or heavy dependencies.

02 / PRINCIPLE

Card-Driven UI

Interfaces should feel visual, intuitive, and natural using structured cards and a clear layout hierarchy.

03 / PRINCIPLE

Local First

Your data belongs on your device. Prioritizing local storage and direct offline functionality.
