Development Philosophy
01 / PRINCIPLE

Zero Bloat

Every line of code should have a reason to exist. No unnecessary background tasks, no excessive dependencies, and no features added simply for the sake of adding them.

02 / PRINCIPLE

Card-Driven UI

Interfaces should feel visual, intuitive, and natural to navigate. Z Apps use structured cards and clear hierarchy instead of overwhelming users with dense interfaces.

03 / PRINCIPLE

Local First

Your data belongs on your device. Z Apps prioritize local storage, offline functionality, and direct device interaction instead of unnecessary dependence on cloud services.
